About LifeLab

A project exploring evolution through an interactive ecosystem simulation.

Why I Built It

Turning evolution into something you can explore.

Hi there, I am a student who will be taking AP Biology this year. I was just doing my summer work and I found about Genes and Evolutions. So I decided to make something which I can actually help me understand these things. I am an interactive learner and this is great simulation. I would say it is not the best out there but it is something plus the chatbot can explain things. I hope you guys liked it!
