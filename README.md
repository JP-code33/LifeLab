# LifeLab

LifeLab is an interactive scosystem simulation. It is made for you to observe organisms evolve over time. They have different traits like speed, size, and vision. It affects how they find food, reproduce and survive. Over time there is slight mutations.

# How It Works

There are 3 traits. Speed can let the organisms move at a different rates of speed. Size can be varying from creature to creature. Vision lets see organisms far away or short. Energy usage depends on how powerful the trait of the organism is. If they have higher speed, bigger size and long vision it cost them more energy to survive. They inherit their parent's trait with a small mutation. Over time the average changes which is visible in live changing graphs.

# How To Run An Experiment

It is very simple. You open the [webpage]([url](https://lifelab-project.netlify.app/guide)) and you can read about the simulation in detail or jump straight into the simulation. Then you can choose the starting food availability, food regeneration speed and the starting population. Then you simply click on the start simulation button and watch those creatures. That is not it. Then you can scroll down and view the lie changing graphs and see how those starting independent variables are the affecting the survival process. If you want to save the experiment then you have to click "End Experiment" button otherwise it will not save the experiment. Then if you have enough runs then you can choose different experiments (they are numbered) and compare them by yourself or you can just ask the AI chatbot "LifeLab Analyst" to give you an scientific reasoning behind the results or patterns.
