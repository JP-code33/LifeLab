About The Simulation

What is LifeLab?

LifeLab is an interactive ecosystem simulation where organisms compete for resources and pass traits to future generations

Each organism has a different characteristics that affect how successfully it can survive and reprodcue

How It Works?

Evoultion in LifeLab

Organisms have traits such as speed, size and vision. These traits affect how well they find food, survive, reproduce.

Over time, successful traits can become more common as new generations appear.
